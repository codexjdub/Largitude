Largitude test page

Each card is one kind of image Largitude should handle, or should leave alone. Hover over the image in a card for a moment: a green FULL image means Largitude found the full-size version, a gray or amber one means it showed a smaller one. Run checks hovers each card for you and lists what happened; click it, then move the mouse off the page (onto the tab bar, say) until it's done, since a real mouse over the page interferes. Open this page through npm run serve (http://127.0.0.1:8766/test/test-page.html) so the frame card works; add ?standalone to try it without the extension, in any browser.

By hand

  1. With a popup open, move off the image: it closes. Move back: it opens again after a moment.
  2. With a popup open, press Escape or click: it closes and stays closed until you move off the image and back.
  3. With a popup open, scroll: it closes.
  4. On the Reddit gallery, scrolling down (the way that scrolls the page down) shows the next image, and one swipe on a trackpad moves one image, however long its momentum lasts. At the last image, the next swipe scrolls the page and closes the popup.
  5. With a popup open, press S: Firefox saves the full-size image (asking where first, if it's set to), and the popup says "Saved". With a gallery, it saves the image the popup shows at the time.
  6. On a video card, the popup plays the video muted and looping, with a bar along the bottom showing how far it has played once the browser knows how long the video is (the test video doesn't say, so the bar may not show, or only after a while). M says "No sound" (the test video has none), Space pauses and plays, and S saves the video.
  7. Click in this text box, which is inside a web component as comment boxes often are, then hover an image and type: S, Space and the arrow keys go into the box, not to the popup.
  8. Add 127.0.0.1 under Sites to leave alone in Largitude's settings, then hover here again without reloading: nothing shows. Remove it, and the popups are back.
