Largitude shows the full-size version of an image when you rest the mouse on it: a thumbnail, a picture in a post, or a link to an image file. The popup opens beside the pointer, fitted to the window, and closes when you move away.

• Finds the largest version a page offers: a link to the image file, the biggest of the sizes the page lists, or the image itself.
• Knows where Reddit and X keep the original uploads, so you see the full image rather than the page's smaller copy.
• Galleries: on a Reddit gallery or an X post with several photos, the arrow keys or the mouse wheel move through them, with a counter showing where you are.
• Press S to save the image the popup shows.
• Sees through the transparent layers many sites put over their images, and into web components.
• Opens at its final size right away, with the thumbnail standing in while the full image loads.
• Stays out of the way: the mouse goes right through it, it skips images already at full size and small icons, and a click, Escape or scrolling closes it.
• Settings: sites to leave alone, and which way the wheel moves through a gallery. Changes apply at once.

Largitude collects no data and sends nothing anywhere. It only loads the images the page refers to, or larger versions of them from the same image service.
